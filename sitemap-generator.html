FAQ

Common Questions

Everything about XML sitemaps, crawling, and getting indexed by Google faster.

Why does the crawl fail on some sites?

Crawling uses a public CORS proxy to fetch pages. Some sites block requests from proxies, return login pages, or use JavaScript-only rendering that can't be parsed as HTML. For these sites use Manual Mode — paste your URLs directly.

What is crawl depth and which should I choose?

Depth 1 only fetches your homepage and extracts links from it. Depth 2 then visits each of those links and extracts their links. Depth 3 goes one level deeper. For most sites Depth 2 finds all important pages. Use Depth 3 for large sites with deep content structures.

Where do I put my sitemap.xml file?

Upload sitemap.xml to your website root so it's accessible at https://yoursite.com/sitemap.xml. Then submit that URL in Google Search Console under Sitemaps. Also add this line to your robots.txt: Sitemap: https://yoursite.com/sitemap.xml

Should I include every page in my sitemap?

No — exclude pages with noindex tags, duplicate content, admin pages, login pages, and URL parameter variations. Only include pages you want Google to index. Quality over quantity improves crawl budget efficiency.
